Typography options for Theme Park Press

Each card shows the header, a book title and author line, body text, and a spine. All are free Google Fonts.

To adopt one, change three variables in src/press/press.css (--fontDisplay, --fontHeadline, --fontText), the Google Fonts link in index.html, and FONT in src/press/theme.ts (the covers and spines are drawn on canvas with the same face). A display face can be paired with a quieter text face: for example Fraunces or Bodoni Moda for titles with Newsreader or Spectral for body text.
