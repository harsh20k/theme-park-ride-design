Typography options for Theme Park Press

36 pairings across seven very different families. The top line of each card names the face used for titles, covers and spines, then the face used for reading text. Type your own title to test long and short ones.

Titles, the header, covers and spines use the first face; the 543 pages of reading text use the second. Loud display faces work as titles but should stay paired with a plain text face. To adopt a pairing: --fontDisplay / --fontHeadline / --fontText in src/press/press.css, the Google Fonts link in index.html, and FONT in src/press/theme.ts (covers and spines are painted with it).
