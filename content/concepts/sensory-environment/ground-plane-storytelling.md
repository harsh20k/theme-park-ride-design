The ground is the one surface every guest touches continuously and almost nobody looks at, which makes it perfect for quiet storytelling. Imagineer Zach Riddley says the team would rather guests not notice the ground much, yet paving is planned as carefully as facades. At Disney California Adventure, Hollywood Land's red brick simply stops and Avengers Campus's grey block begins, with drains and diamond-plate inserts as accents. The paving is the border.

Three jobs follow from this. First, paving marks [land transitions](../masterplanning/land-transitions.md) and [thresholds and portals](../story-experience/thresholds-and-portals.md) without signage, so a theme change is felt in the feet and the peripheral vision before the mind catches up. Second, it steers movement: a change of pattern or a smoother band nudges [wayfinding](../human-factors/wayfinding.md) and either honours or corrects guests' [desire lines](../human-factors/desire-lines.md). Third, tinted concrete keeps the ground from fighting the scene; Magic Kingdom's Adventureland was repaved in 2021 to restore its original jungle-green tint, a sign of how much the colour mattered to the land's look and how it limits [visual intrusion](../human-factors/visual-intrusion.md) from a plain grey slab. Scoring, aggregate size and even the sound of footsteps can be chosen per land.
