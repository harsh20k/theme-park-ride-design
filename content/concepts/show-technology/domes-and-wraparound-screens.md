Flat screens sit in front of you; a dome surrounds you. When moving imagery fills peripheral vision, the brain's motion system reports that the body is moving even though the seat is still, the effect called [vection](../human-factors/vection.md). That is why Soarin' needs only subtle seat lifts: its vehicle swings riders into an 80-foot, 180-degree concave dome, and the picture does most of the work. In 2015 the Disneyland version swapped its original IMAX film system for a laser-lit digital projector array.

Domes are fed two ways. A single projector with a fisheye lens is simple but limited to one projector's resolution. Several projectors blended edge to edge give far more pixels and can hide out of sight, at the price of regular realignment and colour matching. Avatar Flight of Passage pushes the multi-projector route to 10K, 60 frames per second, in stereo.

Dome design ties directly to the [flying theatre](../ride-systems/flying-theatre.md) and [dome hybrid](../ride-systems/dome-and-screen-hybrids.md) ride types, and to how much [peripheral vision](../human-factors/peripheral-vision-and-speed.md) a seat position actually sees. Projector makers now sell dome packages as a product category.
