A central icon is the tall thing in the middle: Sleeping Beauty Castle, Cinderella Castle, Spaceship Earth, Mount Prometheus at Tokyo DisneySea, or the Pharos Lighthouse at Islands of Adventure. It is a [weenie](../story-experience/weenies.md) at park scale, a visual magnet that pulls guests down Main Street and then tells them where they are from anywhere in the park. In Kevin Lynch's vocabulary it is the city's dominant landmark; in Disney's it is "the beckoning hand".

The icon does several jobs at once. It anchors [wayfinding](../human-factors/wayfinding.md), because losing sight of it is the signal to check a map. It is the park's logo and its default [photo moment](../human-factors/photo-moments.md). At night it becomes the fireworks backdrop and the [lighting](../sensory-environment/night-vs-day.md) focus that holds crowds in the hub until close.

Planning rules follow from this. The icon must be tall enough to clear the berm and rooflines, sit on the axis of the entry, and face the sun so afternoon photos work. Its base is usually a [pinch point](../masterplanning/pinch-points.md), so Imagineers surround it with wide plazas rather than queues.
