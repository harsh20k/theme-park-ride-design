A boat dark ride floats guests through a story on a shallow channel of moving water. Pumps at one end push the flow, the boats drift with it, and gentle bends or an underwater cable keep them spaced. There are no motors on the boat, so it is almost silent, which lets scene audio and music carry the show ([onboard and spatial audio](../show-technology/onboard-and-spatial-audio.md)). Disney refined the century-old Old Mill format for It's a Small World in 1964.

[Pirates of the Caribbean](../history/pirates-of-the-caribbean.md) at Disneyland (1967) is the masterpiece: the same flume system with two drops added, fifty Arrow-built boats of 23 or 24 riders, and over a hundred animatronic figures. Its drops are plain [water-ride hydraulics](../ride-engineering/water-ride-hydraulics.md), a lift and a splash, used as story transitions rather than thrills.

The economics are strong. Continuous loading from a moving dock, big boats and a never-stopping current give some of the highest throughput in any park ([throughput math](../operations-business/throughput-math.md)). Intamin's catalogue slow boat ride offers 8 to 20-seat boats moved by current or cable, indoors or out. The wide, low vantage point also sets a particular [scale and intimacy](../story-experience/scale-and-intimacy.md): sets loom above eye level and water carries reflections and smells.
