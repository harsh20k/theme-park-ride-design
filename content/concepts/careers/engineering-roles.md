Engineering roles turn a sketch into something that can carry people thousands of times a day. Universal Creative's Engineering & Safety area lists engineering, mechatronics, programming and technical project management for ride systems, shows, audio-visual, lighting, special effects and interactives. Typical titles are ride systems engineer, mechanical engineer, structural engineer, electrical and controls engineer, safety engineer and ride programmer.

What they make: [track geometry](../ride-engineering/track-geometry.md) and force studies that keep riders inside the [g-force](../ride-engineering/g-force-basics.md) envelope, structural calculations for supports, control logic for [ride control systems](../safety-standards/ride-control-systems.md), and the test plans used in [testing and commissioning](../design-process/testing-and-commissioning.md). All of it must satisfy standards such as [ASTM F2291](../safety-standards/astm-f2291.md).

Tools are mainstream engineering tools: parametric CAD, finite-element analysis, PLC programming and simulation. Preparation is a mechanical, electrical, civil or mechatronics degree plus hands-on proof. The Ride Engineering Competition gives university teams six months to design and build a working model attraction to a shared prompt; the 2026 event in Hershey drew more than 370 students. Simulators such as NoLimits 2 build intuition for how layout choices feel, and Steve Alcorn's book shows how a ride engineer's week actually looks.
